Signal concepts

Quick reference while experimenting

📈
AmplitudeControls the signal's maximum height. Higher amplitude means a stronger signal.
⏱️
FrequencyHow many cycles occur per second. Higher frequency means more cycles in the same time.
📡
AM modulationThe message signal changes the carrier's amplitude while the carrier frequency stays approximately constant.
Experiment tip: increase modulation depth and watch the envelope become more pronounced.
